This record describes previously read author-page evidence. It does not pretend to be the author's original license text. Original models were already local before the later prohibition on external-service calls and downloads. No further external call is needed to use the local GLBs.

Local conversion: Node.js 24.18.1, installed Three.js 0.186.1 FBXLoader and GLTFExporter. Patient JSON glTF embedded binary was repackaged as GLB with Python's standard library. Skeleton, weights, animation channels and embedded data were preserved. Doctor Idle/Walk tracks were bound by exact matching bone names. Models were not assembled from procedural spheres or ellipses.

Local modifications (2026-10-09, offline only):
Patient: existing continuous skin surface lengthened at legs and exposed neck; skin-aware Head/Neck displacement, inverse-bind/rest bone matrices and animation translations adjusted. Two Loop subdivision rounds with UV/color/skin interpolation, normalized top-four influences, seam-welded smooth normals. Mint/cream clothing and dusty-rose shoes painted as vertex colors from the already-local atlas; source image pixels were not edited. Sitting_Idle leg pose was adjusted to forward thighs and downward shins. The original patient.gltf remains unchanged.
Doctor: per-material skin/hair/eyes/coat/shirt/trouser colors changed locally; two-sided rendering enabled and 98 groups consolidated to six same-material groups. No source geometry was replaced with procedural primitives.
Face portrait: generated by rendering the active patient GLB in local Three.js/Chrome to transparent PNG, not by an external image service. Previous otter PNG concepts are archived and are not active body/portrait assets.
These models are stylized art, not verified standard clinical anatomy. Local asset preview checks do not establish final room behavior or clinical effectiveness.
