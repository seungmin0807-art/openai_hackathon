ORIGINAL CLINIC FURNITURE, EQUIPMENT GEOMETRY AND MATERIALS
License: CC0-1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
Original geometry authored locally with Blender Python for the Mori clinic project.
No external generation service, downloaded model, image texture, or external API.
Generated assets: clinic-station.glb, clinic-chair.glb, stethoscope.glb,
trolley/trolley.glb, instruments/{instruments-tray,otoscope,thermometer,light,syringe,spatula}.glb,
exam-furniture/{exam-bed,stool,visitor-chair,privacy-screen}.glb,
room-furniture/{patient-chair,storage-cabinet,monitor,diagnostic-holder,
instrument-workbench,sink-workcounter}.glb,
room-architecture/{window-curtains,eye-chart,plant}.glb, and their Blender renders.
Additional package license notes use the same CC0 geometry / MIT source licenses.
