# Anatomy asset attribution and license

The locally authored symbolic ear canal and eardrum geometry, material colours and associated review render are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The ear pinna is a derivative of the already-local patient.glb: Quaternius, Rabbit_Bald, Sushi Restaurant Kit (May 2023), CC0. See [clinic/LICENSE-attribution.txt](../../LICENSE-attribution.txt) for the existing local attribution record. The existing game's detachedRabbitEar function baked one ear's skinned surface in its Sitting_Idle pose. The original character file was read only. The head-attachment fragments at the extraction base were trimmed below local glTF Y=.105 and the cut base was capped. The retained pinna was translated downward by .125 while preserving its source surface shape and white/pink vertex colours. The small connected cutaway canal and symbolic curved eardrum are new locally authored CC0 geometry. The resulting ear asset and review render are CC0-1.0.

No external meshes or textures were downloaded for this work. No generated-media services, paid APIs or external model generation were used. Blender and Blender MCP are development tools installed outside the repository.
